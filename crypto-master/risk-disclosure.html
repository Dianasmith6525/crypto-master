Risk Disclosure Statement

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

⚠️ IMPORTANT: Cryptocurrency trading involves substantial risk of loss. You may lose 100% or more of your investment. This document outlines significant risks. Read carefully before trading.

1. Market Risk

1.1 Price Volatility

Cryptocurrency prices can fluctuate dramatically in short timeframes:

  • Bitcoin has experienced 80%+ declines within single years
  • Altcoins frequently see 50%+ price swings in days
  • Flash crashes can occur due to large trades or technical issues
  • No guaranteed recovery from price declines

1.2 Market Manipulation

  • Pump and dump schemes targeting small-cap cryptocurrencies
  • Large traders ("whales") can significantly move prices
  • Lack of regulation allows manipulative trading practices
  • Social media and influencers can artificially inflate prices

1.3 Liquidity Risk

  • Some cryptocurrencies have limited trading volumes
  • Wide bid-ask spreads on illiquid assets
  • Difficulty selling large positions without price impact
  • Market crashes can eliminate liquidity entirely

2. Technology Risk

2.1 Smart Contract Vulnerabilities

  • Bugs in smart contracts can lead to fund loss
  • Exploits can drain entire projects overnight
  • Code audits don't eliminate all risks
  • New vulnerability types emerge constantly

2.2 Network Issues

  • Blockchain network congestion delays transactions
  • High transaction fees during peak times
  • Transaction failures requiring fee repayment
  • Hard forks can result in unexpected asset splits

2.3 Exchange Technology Risk

  • System outages prevent trading during market moves
  • Website crashes during high-volume periods
  • API failures for automated traders
  • Data loss or withdrawal delays

3. Security Risk

3.1 Hacking and Theft

  • Exchange hacks have resulted in billions in losses
  • Personal account compromise can lead to theft
  • Phishing and social engineering attacks
  • Malware and keyloggers targeting traders

3.2 Private Key Loss

  • Lost private keys mean permanent loss of funds
  • No way to recover lost cryptocurrencies
  • Hardware wallet failures without backups
  • Accidental deletion or destruction of keys

3.3 Counterparty Risk

  • Exchange bankruptcy or closure results in fund loss
  • Funds stored on exchanges are not segregated accounts
  • Limited insurance coverage for digital assets
  • No government protection like FDIC insurance

4. Regulatory Risk

4.1 Regulatory Changes

  • New regulations can drastically reduce asset value
  • Government bans on specific cryptocurrencies
  • Changes in tax treatment affecting profitability
  • Trading restrictions in specific jurisdictions

4.2 Compliance Risk

  • Inadvertent violation of sanctions regulations
  • Use on platforms without proper licensing
  • Transaction reversals due to regulatory actions

5. Operational Risk

5.1 Platform Risk

  • Withdrawal restrictions or delays
  • Unexpected account closures
  • Poor customer service during issues
  • Custody issues with stored assets

5.2 Human Error

  • Accidental transfer to wrong wallet address
  • Entering incorrect trade amounts
  • Using wrong network for token transfer
  • No recovery for user mistakes

6. Liquidity and Redemption Risk

  • Inability to sell positions during market downturns
  • Extreme slippage on large trades
  • Front-running by exchanges or other traders
  • Flash loan attacks affecting prices

7. Leverage and Margin Trading Risk

  • Leverage amplifies both gains and losses
  • Liquidation at unfavorable prices
  • Forced closing of positions without consent
  • Owing money after liquidation (negative equity)
  • Margin requirements can increase unexpectedly

8. Cryptocurrency-Specific Risks

8.1 Project Risk

  • Development team abandonment
  • Failed network upgrades
  • Loss of community support
  • Complete project failure and coin worthlessness

8.2 Blockchain Risk

  • 51% attacks on Proof-of-Work networks
  • Consensus mechanism failures
  • Double-spending attacks
  • Irreversible transactions (no chargebacks)

9. Economic and Geopolitical Risk

  • Macroeconomic downturns affecting crypto prices
  • Central bank policies impacting valuations
  • Geopolitical tensions and wars
  • Economic sanctions affecting blockchain networks
  • Central bank digital currencies (CBDCs) affecting adoption

10. Tax and Legal Risk

  • Complex tax reporting requirements
  • Potential tax audits on trading activity
  • Lost tax deductions due to reporting errors
  • Legal liability for regulatory violations
  • Uncertainty in tax treatment of new assets

11. Leverage and Forced Liquidation

If using leverage or margin:

  • You can lose more than your initial investment
  • Liquidation occurs without advance notice
  • Liquidation prices may be far from market price
  • You remain liable for losses exceeding liquidation value

12. Staking and Yield Risks

  • Slashing penalties for validator misconduct
  • Smart contract failures causing loss of staked funds
  • Yield rates not guaranteed and may decrease
  • Lock-up periods preventing withdrawal during crashes
  • Devaluation reducing yields in USD terms

13. Derivative and Futures Risk

  • Leverage in futures contracts amplifies losses
  • Funding rates can be substantial costs
  • Liquidation cascades during volatility
  • Impermanent loss in liquidity pools

14. Risk Management is Your Responsibility

We provide tools but managing risk is your responsibility:

  • Only invest what you can afford to lose completely
  • Diversify your portfolio
  • Use stop-loss orders to limit losses
  • Never use leverage if inexperienced
  • Keep private keys secure
  • Verify addresses before transactions
  • Use hardware wallets for large holdings
  • Don't share personal information

15. Past Performance

Past performance does not guarantee future results. Cryptocurrency markets are highly volatile and unpredictable. A cryptocurrency that increased in value may fall significantly or go to zero.

16. Forward-Looking Statements

Information about future cryptocurrency prices and market movements are subject to significant uncertainties and should not be relied upon.

17. Acknowledgment

By using our platform, you acknowledge that:

  • You have read and understood this risk disclosure
  • You understand the risks of cryptocurrency trading
  • You may lose 100% of your investment
  • You accept full responsibility for your trading decisions
  • You will not hold us liable for market losses
