Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about cryptocurrency trading and our platform

What is cryptocurrency?

Cryptocurrency is a digital form of currency secured by cryptography. Bitcoin, created in 2009, is the first and most well-known cryptocurrency. It operates on a decentralized network called blockchain.

Is cryptocurrency trading risky?

Yes, cryptocurrency markets are highly volatile. Prices can fluctuate significantly in short periods. Never invest more than you can afford to lose. Start with small amounts and educate yourself before trading.

What fees do you charge?

We offer competitive trading fees starting at 0.1% for makers and 0.15% for takers. Deposit and withdrawal fees vary by payment method. Premium members receive discounted rates.

How do I secure my account?

Enable 2FA using Google Authenticator or SMS verification. Use a strong, unique password with uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols. Never share your private keys or recovery phrases.

Can I withdraw my funds anytime?

Yes, you can withdraw your funds 24/7. Withdrawal processing times depend on the blockchain network and payment method chosen (typically 1-24 hours).

What cryptocurrencies do you support?

We support 50+ cryptocurrencies including Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Litecoin (LTC), Ripple (XRP), and many others. New cryptocurrencies are added regularly.
