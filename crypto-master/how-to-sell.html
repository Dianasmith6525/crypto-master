How to Sell Your Cryptocurrency

Convert your crypto holdings to fiat currency with our easy-to-use selling interface.

Step 1: Log Into Your Account

Sign in to your trading account with your email and password. Ensure you have two-factor authentication enabled for security.

Step 2: Navigate to Your Portfolio

Go to the Portfolio or Holdings section in your dashboard. You'll see all your cryptocurrency holdings displayed with current market prices and your investment details.

Step 3: Select the Coin to Sell

Click on the coin you want to sell. The system will show you:

  • Current balance in your wallet
  • Current market price
  • Available balance (after any pending transactions)
  • Total value in USD or your local currency

Step 4: Choose Your Sell Order Type

Market Order (Recommended for quick sales):

  • Sell immediately at the current market price
  • Transaction completes within seconds
  • Best for emergency sales or urgent needs

Limit Order (Recommended for specific prices):

  • Set a specific price at which you want to sell
  • Order waits until price reaches your target
  • Can be active for days or weeks
  • Better for maximizing profits at desired price points

Step 5: Enter the Amount to Sell

Specify how much of the cryptocurrency you want to sell. You can enter:

  • Specific coin amount (e.g., 0.5 BTC)
  • USD equivalent (e.g., $1000)
  • Percentage of your holding (e.g., 50% of your ETH)

Step 6: Review and Confirm

Before finalizing, review:

  • Amount of crypto being sold
  • Expected proceeds in fiat currency
  • Transaction fees
  • Estimated completion time

Step 7: Receive Your Funds

After your sale is executed:

  • Funds appear in your account balance immediately (for market orders)
  • Withdraw to your linked bank account within 1-3 business days
  • Choose cash out via card or bank transfer

Tax Considerations

Keep these tax tips in mind when selling cryptocurrency:

  • Record all transactions - we provide transaction history exports
  • Calculate capital gains - difference between purchase and sale price
  • Short-term vs Long-term gains - rates depend on holding period
  • Report on tax returns - check local tax laws in your country
  • Keep documentation - save receipts and transaction details

Selling Strategy Tips

  • Profit Taking: Sell a portion when reaching profit targets
  • Dollar-Cost Averaging Out: Sell gradually to reduce market impact
  • Rebalancing: Sell high performers to maintain portfolio balance
  • Tax-Loss Harvesting: Sell losses to offset gains
  • Emergency Needs: Quick market sales for immediate cash

Trading Fees

Our selling fees are transparent and competitive:

  • Maker Fee: 0.1%
  • Taker Fee: 0.15%
  • Withdrawal Fee: 0.5% - 1% depending on method
