What is Cryptocurrency?

Cryptocurrency is a digital or virtual currency that uses cryptography for security. Unlike traditional currencies issued by governments, cryptocurrencies are typically decentralized and based on blockchain technology.

Key Characteristics:

  • Decentralized: Not controlled by any government or financial institution
  • Secure: Uses advanced cryptographic techniques to ensure security
  • Transparent: All transactions are recorded on a public ledger (blockchain)
  • Immutable: Once recorded, transactions cannot be altered
  • 24/7 Trading: Markets operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week

Understanding Market Terms

Market Cap:
Total market capitalization = Price × Circulating Supply
Volume:
Total trading volume in the last 24 hours
Volatility:
Degree of price fluctuation - higher volatility means greater price swings
ATH (All Time High):
The highest price a cryptocurrency has ever reached
ATL (All Time Low):
The lowest price a cryptocurrency has ever reached
