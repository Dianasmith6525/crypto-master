Custody & Asset Protection Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

1. Overview

Cryptocurrency Trading Platform prioritizes the security and protection of customer assets. This document outlines our comprehensive custody and asset protection mechanisms.

2. Asset Custody Model

2.1 Custody Arrangement

Customer cryptocurrencies are held in custody on behalf of customers. Assets are held in segregated wallets that are not commingled with company assets.

2.2 Private Key Management

  • Private keys are managed using institutional-grade solutions
  • Multiple signatures required for fund movement
  • Keys are never stored on internet-connected systems
  • Backup keys maintained in secure locations

3. Cold Storage Infrastructure

3.1 Cold Wallet Storage

95% of customer assets are stored in cold storage wallets offline:

  • Offline hardware wallets not connected to the internet
  • Multi-signature cold wallets requiring multiple approvals
  • Geographically distributed storage locations
  • Climate-controlled security vaults
  • Regular backup verification and testing

3.2 Hot Wallet Management

5% of assets maintained in hot wallets for operational liquidity:

  • Real-time monitoring and anomaly detection
  • Daily withdrawal limit controls
  • Advanced firewall and network security
  • Intrusion prevention systems
  • Automated security alerts

4. Security Measures

4.1 Multi-Signature Wallets

Most wallets require multiple signatures (3-of-5 or similar):

  • No single individual can access funds
  • Requires coordination of multiple parties
  • Prevents insider theft or unauthorized access
  • Provides transparency and accountability

4.2 Encryption Standards

  • AES-256 encryption for all stored data
  • RSA-4096 for key storage
  • TLS 1.3 for all communications
  • Encrypted key shards with Shamir's Secret Sharing

4.3 Access Controls

  • Role-based access controls
  • Two-factor authentication for all operations
  • Biometric authentication where available
  • Time-lock mechanisms on sensitive operations
  • Complete audit trails for all access

5. Physical Security

  • Vault-grade security facilities
  • 24/7 video surveillance
  • Armed security personnel
  • Restricted access with biometric entry
  • Environmental monitoring (temperature, humidity)
  • Earthquake and fire protection systems

6. Insurance Coverage

6.1 Crypto Asset Insurance

Customer assets are covered by insurance:

  • Coverage up to $250 million per incident
  • Coverage for theft and hacking
  • Coverage for employee fraud
  • Global coverage across all jurisdictions
  • No insurance deductibles for covered events

6.2 Cyber Insurance

  • Comprehensive cyber liability coverage
  • Coverage for data breaches
  • Business interruption insurance
  • Network security liability

7. Disaster Recovery

7.1 Business Continuity Plan

  • Redundant systems and backups
  • Geographically distributed infrastructure
  • Automatic failover systems
  • Regular disaster recovery testing
  • Recovery time objective (RTO): < 4 hours
  • Recovery point objective (RPO): < 1 hour

7.2 Data Backup

  • Real-time data replication
  • Multiple backup locations
  • Encrypted backups
  • Regular backup integrity verification
  • Recovery testing performed quarterly

8. Liquidity Management

8.1 Fund Availability

We maintain sufficient liquidity to process:

  • All customer withdrawal requests
  • Peak withdrawal demand periods
  • Market emergency scenarios
  • Operational contingencies

8.2 Reserve Requirements

  • 100% of customer fiat balances in segregated accounts
  • 100% of customer cryptocurrency holdings
  • Additional emergency reserves (15% of AUM)
  • Regular compliance audits

9. Withdrawal Procedures

9.1 Withdrawal Authorization

  • Customer-initiated withdrawal requests
  • Verification of customer identity
  • Confirmation of destination address
  • Verification of withdrawal amount

9.2 Fraud Prevention

  • Machine learning fraud detection
  • Unusual activity monitoring
  • Withdrawal limits on new accounts
  • Address whitelist requirements
  • Email and SMS confirmation codes

9.3 Processing Timeline

  • Most withdrawals processed within 24 hours
  • Some blockchain confirmations may add time
  • Bank transfers may take 1-3 business days
  • Customer notified of any delays

10. Audit and Compliance

10.1 Third-Party Audits

  • Annual SOC 2 Type II audits
  • Regular penetration testing
  • Quarterly custody audits
  • Annual security assessments
  • Reserve verification audits

10.2 Internal Controls

  • Segregation of duties
  • Authorization levels
  • Exception reporting
  • Regular reconciliation
  • Internal audit program

11. Bankruptcy Protection

11.1 Customer Asset Segregation

Customer cryptocurrencies are:

  • Legally segregated from company assets
  • Not available to company creditors
  • Protected in bankruptcy proceedings
  • Directly recoverable by customers

11.2 Legal Structure

  • Assets held in trust for customer benefit
  • Company acts as custodian, not owner
  • Bankruptcy remoteness structure
  • Regular legal review

12. Incident Response

12.1 Security Incident Protocol

  • Immediate incident detection and isolation
  • Investigation team activation
  • Law enforcement notification if required
  • Customer notification (within 24 hours)
  • Insurance claim processing
  • Root cause analysis and remediation

12.2 Customer Compensation

If customer funds are lost due to our actions:

  • Insurance covers replacement of lost funds
  • Swift compensation process
  • No customer liability
  • Regulatory authority coordination

13. Transparency and Reporting

13.1 Customer Reporting

  • Real-time account balance visibility
  • Transaction history and confirmation
  • Custody confirmation statements
  • Monthly asset reports

13.2 Public Disclosure

  • Annual security reports published
  • Custody arrangements disclosed
  • Insurance coverage details available
  • Audit results shared with customers

14. Policy Updates

This policy is reviewed annually and updated as needed. Significant changes will be communicated to customers with 30 days' notice.
